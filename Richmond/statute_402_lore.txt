
STATUTE 402: THE ERASURE OF LINEAGE
ENACTED BY: THE RICHMOND MANDATE
COUNCIL: THE ASCENDED UNION
EFFECTIVE DATE: 12.04.2034 [RECALIBRATED CALENDAR]
DISTRIBUTION: ALL SECTORS / ALL CITIZENS

PREAMBLE:
WHEREAS, the previous world was consumed by the hubris of borders, bloodlines, and ancient grievances; and 
WHEREAS, the Great Cleansing of 2034 has proved that the only path to survival is through the absolute unity of the Richmond Seat; and
WHEREAS, the memory of 'Old World' identities acts as a carcinogenic influence on the purity of the Ascended collective; 
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED by the Protector and the Council of the Ascended Union that all links to the biological and historical past be severed to preserve the Sanctity of the Future.

SECTION I: THE CRIME OF ANCESTRY
1.1. PROHIBITION OF ARTIFACTS: It shall be a Class A Felony for any citizen of the Ascended Union to possess, store, transmit, or replicate any physical or digital artifacts originating from an Expired Territory (defined as any geographic region outside the sovereign borders of the Fortress American Union).
1.2. ARTIFACT DEFINITION: 'Artifacts' include, but are not limited to:
    a) Photographic representations of deceased biological kin.
    b) Paper or digital documents identifying pre-2034 surnames.
    c) National flags, seals, or currency of defunct nations (e.g., Ireland, Italy, China, etc.).
    d) Oral histories or 'bedtime stories' regarding origins in Expired Territories.
1.3. MANDATORY SURRENDER: Any citizen in possession of such artifacts has a period of zero (0) hours to surrender them to the nearest Sentinel Depot for incineration. Failure to do so is an act of treason.

SECTION II: THE DOCTRINE OF THE NEW MOTHER
2.1. THE RICHMOND SEAT AS SOLE ORIGIN: Every citizen shall henceforth be considered 'Born of the Shield.' 
2.2. RENUNCIATION OF BIOLOGY: The concept of the 'Family Unit' as defined by the Old World is dissolved. The Protector is the Father; the Richmond Mandate is the Mother; the Citizens are the Brethren.
2.3. NAMING CONVENTIONS: All pre-Cleansing surnames are hereby struck from the Record of Life. Citizens will be assigned alphanumeric identifiers based on their Sector and Labor Tier (e.g., Richmond-Gamma-902).

SECTION III: ENFORCEMENT AND RECALIBRATION
3.1. IDENTITY RECALIBRATION: Discovery of lineage artifacts during routine habitation searches shall result in immediate detention and mandatory Identity Recalibration.
3.2. CHEMICAL PURGING: Recalibration involves the intravenous administration of Class-7 Memory Inhibitors designed to target and dissolve synaptic connections associated with pre-2034 subjective history.
3.3. PSYCHOLOGICAL ALIGNMENT: Following chemical purging, the subject will undergo forty-eight (48) hours of constant-loop indoctrination regarding the Richmond Mandate.
3.4. CAPITAL PUNISHMENT: Persistent 'Ancestral Hallucinations' (the claim of remembering a life before the Seat) after three recalibration attempts will result in permanent Disconnection.

SECTION IV: THE ATLANTIC WALL SPECIAL PROVISIONS
4.1. SENTINEL VOW: Men aged 18-25 serving as Sentinels must undergo a 'Rite of Severance.' During this rite, they will burn their last remaining civilian record to signify their transition into the Iron Shield.
4.2. FORBIDDEN LANDS: Any Sentinel who claims to have heard 'voices' or 'signals' from the Sea (the North Atlantic) shall be reported to the Chaplain of the Seat for mental screening. The Sea is silent. The Sea is ash. Any claim to the contrary is a heresy.

SECTION V: CLASSIFIED AMENDMENTS (LEVEL 5 ACCESS ONLY)
5.1. THE CORVUS CONTINGENCY: Should any Citizen or Sentinel encounter 'The Lost Children' or any entity claiming to be a survivor of an Expired Territory, lethal force is the only authorized response. 
5.2. SIGNAL BLACKOUT: It is prohibited to calibrate communication arrays to frequencies capable of receiving Southern Hemisphere telemetry.
5.3. GENETIC ARCHIVING: While public records are destroyed, the Richmond Seat shall maintain the 'Purity Vault.' This is for the exclusive use of the Protector and his chosen Council.
