
THE BOOK OF THE ASCENT: THE DECALOGUE AND COMMENTARY
OFFICIAL DOCTRINE OF THE RICHMOND MANDATE
SECTOR 4 COMMUNAL PRAYER EDITION [2084]

I. THE DECALOGUE OF THE ASCENT
(To be recited at 0600 and 1800 daily by all Citizens and Sentinels)

COMMANDMENT I: Thou shalt have no Seat before Richmond.
COMMENTARY: All authority flows from the Richmond Seat. To look for leadership in the ruins of the Old World or the myths of the South is to invite the void. There is only one Throne of Mercy.

COMMANDMENT II: Thou shalt not carve images of the Expired Territories, for they are the shapes of sin.
COMMENTARY: The drawing of maps, the sketching of ancient skylines, and the depiction of pre-Cleansing vistas are heresies. The mind must be a blank slate upon which the Protector writes the Future.

COMMANDMENT III: Thou shalt not take the Protector’s name in doubt, for His breath is thy life.
COMMENTARY: Doubt is the first stage of spiritual rot. To question the Mandate is to question the very oxygen provided by the Seat’s refineries. Silence is the highest form of worship.

COMMANDMENT IV: Remember the Day of Cleansing, and keep it holy through thy labor.
COMMENTARY: 12.04.2034 was not an ending, but a filtering. We work because we were chosen to remain. Every brick laid in the Wall is a prayer made manifest.

COMMANDMENT V: Honor the Mandate and the Wall, that thy days may be long upon the Sanctified Soil.
COMMENTARY: The Wall is thy mother and thy father. It protects the womb of Richmond from the radioactive filth of the Dead Zones. To disrespect the Wall is to orphan thy soul.

COMMANDMENT VI: Thou shalt not seek the Ghost-Kin; thy lineage began in the fire of 2034.
COMMENTARY: You have no ancestors. You have no bloodline. The fire of the Cleansing cauterized the past. Any claim to biological heritage is a hallucination of the Hubris.

COMMANDMENT VII: Thou shalt not commit Curiosity; the horizon is a lure for the weak of spirit.
COMMENTARY: The horizon is where the world ends. To look upon it with longing is to desire the Ash. Keep thy eyes upon thy labor and thy heart upon the Seat.

COMMANDMENT VIII: Thou shalt not steal data from the Forbidden Archives, for truth is a gift, not a right.
COMMENTARY: Knowledge is a ration, distributed by the Protector according to thy need. To seek unauthorized data is to consume poison.

COMMANDMENT IX: Thou shalt not bear witness to the 'Green Myths' of the South.
COMMENTARY: The 'Cape Town Signal' is a siren song of the Deceiver. There is no green in the South. There is only a different shade of grey. To listen is to be disconnected.

COMMANDMENT X: Thou shalt covet only the Purity of the Ascended.
COMMENTARY: Desire nothing but the advancement of thy Labor Tier and the eventual uploading of thy consciousness to the Great Archive of the Seat.

II. THE DAILY BENEDICTION
May the Wall be high. May the Ash stay low. 
May the Protector’s eyes find no shadows in my heart.
If I fail the Seat, may the Seat find me useful for parts.
