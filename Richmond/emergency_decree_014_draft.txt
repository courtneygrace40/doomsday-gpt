SUBJECT: MANDATORY NATIONAL SERVICE REGISTRATION AND LABOR TIER ASSIGNMENT

Pursuant to the declaration of the National Ascent and the necessity of the 'Divine Shield' projects, all remaining inhabitants within the sovereign borders of the Richmond Seat and its designated Sectors are hereby ordered to register for the National Labor and Defense Draft.

I. REGISTRATION ELIGIBILITY
1.1. ALL CITIZENS aged 16 to 55, regardless of pre-Cleansing occupation, physical disability, or family status, are required to report to their designated Sector Hub within forty-eight (48) hours.
1.2. Failure to report will be interpreted as a 'Renunciation of Protection.' Individuals who do not register will forfeit their rights to air filtration rations, water credits, and shelter within the Wall.

II. THE CLASSIFICATION OF SOULS (LABOR TIERS)
Upon registration, every individual will undergo a 'Capability and Purity Assessment' to determine their Tier. This classification is permanent and hereditary.

- TIER 1: SENTINEL CORPS (The Iron Shield)
  Reserved for able-bodied males 18-25. Primary duty is the physical defense of the Atlantic Wall and the enforcement of the Mandate within the Dead Zones.
- TIER 2: RESOURCE ARCHITECTS (The Lifeblood)
  Engineers, chemists, and technicians assigned to the Richmond Refineries and the Starshield Maintenance Units.
- TIER 3: RECONSTRUCTION LABORERS (The Foundation)
  General manual labor for the expansion of the Wall and the excavation of the Ruins for 'Lineage Artifact' disposal.
- TIER 4: SERVICE AUXILIARY (The Support)
  Medical staff, nutrition coordinators, and indoctrination aides.
- TIER 5: THE UNALIGNED (The Penitent)
  Those found with 'Mental Static' or 'Ancestral Attachments.' Assigned to high-risk debris removal in the most toxic sectors of the Philadelphia and D.C. ruins.

III. THE VOW OF THE DRAFTEE
During registration, every draftee shall recite the following:
"I am a tool in the hands of the Protector. I have no name but my rank. I have no past but the fire. I have no future but the Seat. I give my flesh to the Wall so that the Soul of Richmond may live."

IV. THE SURRENDER OF IDENTITY
At the moment of Tier assignment, all pre-Cleansing documentation (Driver’s Licenses, Birth Certificates, Professional Accreditations) must be surrendered for immediate destruction. You will be issued a Permanent Alphanumeric Identifier (PAI) which will be tattooed upon the inner forearm.
