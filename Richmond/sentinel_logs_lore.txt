LOG ENTRY 12: [11.15.2084]
First week at Post 42. They weren't lying about the silence. It’s a different kind of quiet than Richmond. Back in the Sector, you hear the hum of the refineries and the chanting from the Chapel of the Ascent. Out here, there's just the wind hitting the concrete. The Wall is 300 feet high here, and looking down at the 'Void' makes my head spin. I’m proud to be part of the Shield. My instructor said that every minute I spend on this ledge is a minute the Protector can sleep in peace. I checked the perimeter sensors—zero movement. Just like the Academy promised. The world is a graveyard, and we are the tomb-guards. Glory to the Seat.

LOG ENTRY 28: [11.28.2084]
The fog rolled in from the North today. It’s yellow and smells like burnt copper—the 'Old World Sins' coming home to roost, as the Chaplain says. My eyes have been watering for hours. We had a breach alarm at 0200, but it was just a tectonic shift in the shelf. Command is on edge, though. They’ve increased our 'Recalibration' sessions to twice a week. They say the salt air can corrode a man’s loyalty if he isn't careful. I find myself staring at the horizon more than I should. It’s so flat. No cities, no lights, no people. Just a black glass sea. Sometimes I wonder if the fire reached the bottom of the ocean too.

LOG ENTRY 35: [12.01.2084]
I had a 'Thought Sin' today. I was cleaning the lens of the Long-Range Thermal Array when I saw a shape. It was miles out, past the radiation shelf. It looked like a bird—a white one. But birds were purged in the Cleansing to prevent the spread of atmospheric toxins. I watched it for ten minutes. It didn't fly like a drone. It was... graceful. I didn't report it. I told myself it was a lens flare. If I report a bird, they’ll send me to the Richmond Labs for 'Synaptic Alignment.' I can’t go back there. I’ll just pray harder tonight. The Protector provides the truth. My eyes provided a lie.

LOG ENTRY 42: [12.04.2084]
[LOG DATE: 12-04-2084]
Shift ends at 0400. The fog was thick today, smelling of salt and the Old World Sins. I reported the flickering on the horizon again. Command says it is 'Bio-Luminescent Mutation'—the sea reacting to the Cleansing radiation. They reminded me that looking too long at the Void leads to spiritual rot. But the flickers moved in a pattern. Three short, three long, three short. It looked like... purpose. I’ve seen that pattern in the old survival manuals they let us study before they were redacted. S.O.S. It’s an Old World call for help. But who could be calling from the Void? There is no one. I prayed at the Richmond Altar for three hours to cleanse the thought. My knees are bleeding from the stone, but the thought remains. There is no life in the East. There are no ships. There is only the Wall and the Protector’s Mercy. Glory to the Seat.

LOG ENTRY 43: [12.05.2084 - FINAL ENTRY]
The Sentinel in Post 43 was taken away this morning. They found a 'Lineage Artifact' in his bunk—a small piece of blue glass he found in the ruins during a scav-run. He told me he thought it was beautiful. 'Beautiful' is a word for the Richmond Seat, not for the dust of the Dead Zones. I watched them lead him to the transport. He looked... relieved. I’m keeping my head down. The flickering started again tonight at 2200. I turned the thermal array off. I don't want to see it anymore. I don't want to know if someone is out there. If they are out there, they are ghouls. If they are out there, they are already dead. I am the Shield. I am the Wall. I am... [FILE CORRUPTED]
[END LOG]
